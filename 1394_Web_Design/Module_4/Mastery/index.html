Welcome to My Paint Lab!

I am so glad you stopped by! I’m Annie, a proud Panamanian artist inspired by the color, rhythm, culture, and corazón of my home country. My family means everything to me, including my mom, two sisters, brother, and my sweet cat Mini May. I have a degree in English, and I love hiking, karaoke, guitar, violin, and painting. Annie’s Paint Lab is where I turn life, love, and a little Latin sazón into custom art made with joy.

Custom Wearable Art

Art is not only for walls; it can be worn too. At Annie’s Paint Lab, I create custom designs on clothing, hats, and other wearable pieces that help people show their personality in a bold and creative way. Each piece is hand-painted with color, care, and a little Latin spice.

Canvas Creations!

Every canvas tells a story. I create colorful custom paintings inspired by music, culture, nature, emotions, and personal ideas. Whether it is a gift, home decoration, or a special memory, each canvas is made to feel personal, meaningful, and full of life.

Let's Create Something Beautiful

Annie’s Paint Lab is where imagination, color, and creativity come together. Whether you want a custom canvas, a painted clothing piece, or a unique gift, I would love to help bring your idea to life. Thank you for visiting my Paint Lab, and I hope my art brings a little joy, color, and corazón to your day.
